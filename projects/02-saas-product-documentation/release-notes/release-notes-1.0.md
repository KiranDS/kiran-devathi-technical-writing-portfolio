# SupportFlow release notes — version 1.0

**Release date:** Illustrative  
**Documentation version:** 1.0

## Overview

This initial portfolio release introduces the core SupportFlow product concepts and workflows. SupportFlow is fictional; these notes describe the capabilities represented in the sample documentation, not a deployed product release.

## Capabilities represented

### Ticket management
- Create and maintain customer support tickets.
- Assign tickets to teams and individual agents.
- Track ticket status and priority.
- Record internal notes and customer responses.

### Queue management
- Review shared team work.
- Filter tickets by common attributes.
- Monitor ownership and outstanding work.

### SLA management
- Define illustrative first-response and resolution targets.
- Associate policies with business-hour schedules.
- Review tickets approaching or exceeding targets.

### User and team administration
- Represent Administrator, Team Lead, and Agent roles.
- Organise users into teams.
- Describe basic access-review practices.

### Reporting
- Define common operational metrics.
- Provide illustrative dashboard examples.
