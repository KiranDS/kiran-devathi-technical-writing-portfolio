# Frequently asked questions

**Audience:** All SupportFlow users  
**Product version:** 1.0

> SupportFlow is a fictional portfolio product. These answers describe the illustrative product model used in this documentation set.

## General

### What is SupportFlow?
SupportFlow is a fictional customer support platform for organising customer requests, managing ticket workflows, monitoring service targets, and reviewing support performance.

### Is SupportFlow a live service?
No. It is a simulated product created to demonstrate professional SaaS documentation capabilities.

### What is a workspace?
A workspace is an organisation-specific area containing its users, teams, tickets, and configuration.

## Tickets

### What is a ticket?
A ticket is a record of a customer request. It contains information such as the subject, description, requester, status, priority, owner, and activity history.

### What do the ticket statuses mean?
- **New:** created and awaiting active handling.
- **Open:** actively being worked.
- **Pending:** waiting for an external response or action.
- **Resolved:** the support team has recorded a resolution.

### What is the difference between an internal note and a customer response?
An internal note is intended for authorised support staff. A customer response is intended for the requester. Always verify the communication type before saving.

### Can I change a ticket's assignee?
The illustrative product permits assignment workflows for authorised roles. Your actual permissions depend on workspace configuration.

## SLAs and reporting

### What is an SLA?
An SLA is a defined target for a support activity, such as first response or resolution.

### Does Pending status pause an SLA?
This depends on the configured policy. The portfolio example does not define a universal pause rule.

### What does SLA attainment mean?
It is the share of eligible tickets that meet the applicable SLA target, based on the reporting definition.

## Access and administration

### Why can I not see Administration?
Your role may not include workspace-administration permissions. Contact your workspace administrator.

### Who can invite users?
The illustrative access model assigns user management to Administrators.

### Where can I find help for an access problem?
See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) or contact your workspace administrator.

## Data and security

### What security certifications does SupportFlow have?
No certifications are claimed. SupportFlow is a fictional portfolio product, and this documentation does not provide evidence of any certification.

### How long is customer data retained?
No retention period is specified in this portfolio example. A real product's retention policy must be documented from approved product and legal sources.
