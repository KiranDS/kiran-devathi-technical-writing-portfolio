# Getting started with SupportFlow

**Audience:** New support agents and workspace administrators  
**Product version:** 1.0

## Before you begin

You need:
- A SupportFlow workspace address supplied by your organisation.
- An account created or invited by a workspace administrator.
- A supported browser and an internet connection.

SupportFlow is fictional in this portfolio. The steps describe an illustrative onboarding flow; actual screens and authentication behaviour would need validation against a running product.

## 1. Access your workspace

1. Open the workspace address provided by your administrator.
2. Enter the email address associated with your SupportFlow account.
3. Complete the sign-in process configured by your organisation.
4. If prompted, complete any additional identity-verification step.
5. Confirm that the workspace name shown after sign-in matches your organisation.

**Expected result:** The SupportFlow workspace opens and displays the navigation options available to your role.

## 2. Understand the home page

The home page may contain:
- **Inbox:** tickets assigned to you.
- **Queues:** tickets available to your team.
- **Reports:** metrics available to your role.
- **Knowledge base:** support articles.
- **Administration:** workspace settings, if you have administrator access.

The items displayed depend on your assigned role and workspace configuration.

## 3. Review your inbox

1. Select **Inbox**.
2. Review the ticket subject, priority, status, and last-updated time.
3. Select a ticket to open its details.
4. Read the description and activity history before making changes.
5. Confirm that the ticket is assigned to you before beginning work.

**Expected result:** You can identify the tickets currently assigned to your account.

## 4. Open a team queue

1. Select **Queues**.
2. Choose a queue available to your team.
3. Apply filters such as status or priority, if available.
4. Open a ticket to review its details.
5. Follow your team's assignment process before taking ownership.

**Note:** A queue is a view of tickets. It is not a separate copy of a ticket.

## 5. Create a ticket

1. Select **New ticket**.
2. Enter the requester's details.
3. Add a concise subject.
4. Enter the request description, including relevant context.
5. Select a priority based on your team's guidelines.
6. Assign the ticket to the appropriate team or agent, if permitted.
7. Save the ticket.

**Expected result:** The new ticket appears in the appropriate queue or inbox.

## 6. Find a knowledge article

1. Open **Knowledge base**.
2. Enter a keyword or phrase describing the issue.
3. Review the article title and summary.
4. Open the most relevant article.
5. Check that the article applies to the customer's product version and situation before sharing its guidance.

## 7. Get help

If you cannot sign in or cannot see an expected workspace area:
1. Confirm that you are using the correct workspace address.
2. Check that you are signing in with the invited email address.
3. Contact your workspace administrator.
4. Include the error message and approximate time of the issue. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for common issues.
