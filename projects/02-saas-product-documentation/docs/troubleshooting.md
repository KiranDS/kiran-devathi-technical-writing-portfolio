# Troubleshooting SupportFlow

**Audience:** All SupportFlow users  
**Product version:** 1.0

## 1. Before troubleshooting

When reporting a problem:
- Record the action you attempted.
- Note the approximate time and time zone.
- Capture the exact error message.
- Record the ticket ID or affected page, if applicable.
- Describe what you expected and what happened.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, payment-card details, or other secrets in a support request.

## 2. Sign-in problems

### I cannot access my workspace

1. Confirm that you are using the workspace address provided by your organisation.
2. Confirm that you are using the invited email address.
3. Check whether your organisation requires an additional identity-verification step.
4. Contact your workspace administrator if access is still unavailable.

**Possible cause:** The account may not have been provisioned, may be inactive, or may be associated with a different workspace.

### I can sign in but cannot see Administration

**Possible cause:** Your assigned role may not include administrator permissions.

**Resolution:** Contact a workspace administrator to confirm your role. Do not request elevated access unless it is required for your responsibilities.

## 3. Ticket problems

### I cannot find a ticket

1. Check the ticket ID, if available.
2. Confirm that you are viewing the correct workspace.
3. Clear or broaden queue filters.
4. Check whether the ticket is assigned to another team.
5. Ask a team lead to confirm your access to the relevant queue.

### I cannot update a ticket

**Possible causes:**
- You do not have permission to edit the ticket.
- Another workflow or policy restricts the action.
- The ticket is in a state that does not allow the requested change.

**Resolution:** Confirm your role and the ticket's current status. Contact a team lead or administrator if the restriction is unexpected.

### A ticket appears in the wrong queue

1. Open the ticket.
2. Review its team, assignee, status, and priority.
3. Confirm the intended routing with the team lead.
4. Update the assignment only if you are authorised.
5. Verify that the ticket appears in the expected queue.

## 4. SLA problems

### An SLA target looks incorrect

1. Confirm the ticket priority.
2. Check the policy that applies to the ticket.
3. Verify the business-hour schedule and time zone.
4. Review status changes and documented pause rules.
5. Contact the administrator if the result remains unexplained.

### A ticket is marked at risk unexpectedly

Review the target time, ticket age, business-hour schedule, and any policy exceptions. Do not change ticket priority solely to alter the displayed SLA state.

## 5. Knowledge base problems

### Search does not return the expected article

- Try a shorter or alternative keyword.
- Check spelling and product terminology.
- Confirm that the article is published and available to your audience.
- Contact the knowledge base owner if the article should be available but is not listed.

## 6. Information to include when escalating

Provide:
- Workspace name (if permitted by policy).
- Ticket ID or affected feature.
- Date, time, and time zone.
- Steps to reproduce.
- Expected result.
- Actual result and exact error text.
- Browser and version, if relevant.
- A screenshot with personal or confidential information redacted.
